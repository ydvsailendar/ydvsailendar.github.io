Content Publisher

An internal publishing tool for KathaNova Studio's own YouTube channels.

Content Publisher uploads the original animated videos we make to the YouTube channels we own, and sets each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail, language and publishing time. It is used only by the channel owner. It has no other users and does not access anyone else's channels or data.
