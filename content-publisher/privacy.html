Privacy policy

Content Publisher · last updated 31 October 2026

Content Publisher is an internal tool that KathaNova Studio uses to publish its own videos to the YouTube channels it owns. This page explains what data the tool accesses and what it does with it.

Who uses it

Only the owner of KathaNova Studio's channels. The tool is not offered to the public, and no other person or organisation can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

When the channel owner signs a channel in, the tool receives permission from Google to:

  • upload videos to that channel (youtube.upload), and
  • manage that channel's own videos and settings, such as titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy and scheduling (youtube).

It reads the channel's name and ID to confirm it is publishing to the right channel. It does not read viewers' personal data, comments from other users, or data from any channel the owner does not control.

How data is stored

The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder readable by that user alone. They are not uploaded to any server, database or third party.

Sharing

The tool does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to anyone. It sends data only to YouTube, to carry out the uploads and edits the owner asks for.

Google API Services

Content Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens stop working, and they can then be deleted from the owner's computer.
